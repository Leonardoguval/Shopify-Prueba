# Velora Organic Content Plan

This plan is designed for Instagram, Facebook and TikTok using static images only, no video.

## Core strategy

Use static images as a visual language of calm, premium lifestyle, and simple everyday comfort. The content should feel like a curated wellness brand, not like a generic dropshipping store.

## Content format 1: Product in context

Use one clean lifestyle image per product.

Examples:
- satin pillowcase on a calm bed
- sleep mask beside a book and lamp
- neck wrap on a sofa during evening relaxation
- lumbar pillow on a modern chair

Caption example:
Small comforts that make a real difference. A softer bedtime routine, a calmer evening, and a more comfortable home setup.

## Content format 2: Problem / solution carousel

Use 4-6 images in a carousel.

Example structure:
1. Problem: restless nights
2. Problem: long workday tension
3. Product solution
4. Product in use
5. Calm bedroom or setup
6. CTA: Shop the collection

Caption example:
When your routine needs a reset, the smallest upgrades can make the biggest difference.

## Content format 3: Benefit-focused carousel

Use 4 slides with a simple structure:
- Benefit 1
- Benefit 2
- Benefit 3
- Product + CTA

Example for sleep mask:
- Blocks light
- Soft feel
- Travel friendly
- Shop now

## Content format 4: Lifestyle mood posts

Use one image with a short text overlay.

Examples:
- Better evenings start with better comfort.
- A calmer routine, made simple.
- Designed for rest, recovery and everyday ease.

## Content format 5: UGC-style static posts

Create image posts that feel like customer reviews or lifestyle snapshots.

Examples:
- “This is now part of my nightly routine.”
- “The kind of small upgrade that actually matters.”
- “Simple comfort that feels worth it.”

## Posting rhythm

Per week:
- 3 static posts
- 2 carousels
- 3 stories
- 1 product spotlight

## Best themes for the content

- calm evenings
- bedroom reset
- work-from-home comfort
- recovery after a long day
- soft luxury lifestyle
- practical wellness

## Caption style

Keep captions:
- short
- calm
- premium
- not overly salesy
- emotionally clear

Good formula:
- hook
- benefit
- soft CTA

Example:
A softer way to wind down. Designed for calmer evenings and a more comforting bedtime routine. Shop the collection.

## Suggested post ideas

1. Better evenings start with better comfort.
2. A small upgrade that changes your routine.
3. The calmest part of your day starts at home.
4. Sleep, recovery, and comfort in one simple edit.
5. A premium feel for everyday routines.
6. Designed for rest, not overstimulation.
7. Comfort that fits naturally into your day.
8. A better routine can start with one simple product.
9. Soft textures. Calm spaces. Better evenings.
10. Beauty in the details of everyday comfort.

## Recommended visual style

- warm neutral tones
- natural light
- soft shadows
- clean backgrounds
- minimal props
- product centered
- premium but not flashy

## What not to do

- do not use overly loud graphics
- do not overuse discount language
- do not make the brand look cheap
- do not post random products without context
- do not try to sound medical
